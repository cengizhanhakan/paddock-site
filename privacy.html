Privacy Policy — Paddock: Horse Racing Game

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Paddock is a single-player, offline game. It does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or contacts.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (tickets, points, season, statistics) and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us.

Advertising

Paddock shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from IP address, and usage data to serve and measure ads. On iOS, tracking for personalized ads happens only if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings. The "Remove ads" purchase disables all ads.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment details.

Leaderboards

If you submit a weekly card score, it is sent to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games under your platform player identity, governed by their privacy policies.

Tickets and points

Tickets and points in Paddock are virtual. They have no monetary value and cannot be purchased, sold, transferred or cashed out. Each race costs one ticket regardless of the result. Tickets refill over time, from daily bonuses, or by watching an optional ad. Points only ever increase.

Children

Paddock is rated 4+. There is no gambling in the game: nothing is wagered, tickets cannot be bought, points cannot be exchanged for anything, and there are no prizes. Paddock is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.
